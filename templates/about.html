Arxiv Resanity

Arxiv Resanity keeps up with arXiv for you. Save papers you like into tags, one per topic you follow, and it ranks every new paper against each of them. "Not for this tag" on a bad match teaches the tag what to leave out. Every Sunday your digest collects the best new papers per tag, also by email if you add your address on your profile.
This instance indexes cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.NE and cs.RO, and you can add any other arXiv paper by link. Rankings come from a small model trained on your tags each time, over tfidf features and EmbeddingGemma embeddings of titles and abstracts.
AI agents such as Claude Code can use the site too: connect one.
Arxiv Resanity is a fork of arxiv-sanity-lite by Andrej Karpathy, MIT licensed.
